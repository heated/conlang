RZ, a Romance zonal language

RZ and its script

RZ is built so that people who already read Spanish, Portuguese, Italian or French can read it without studying it first. Normally it is written in the Latin alphabet. It also has a second, optional script, shown here under each word. In that script the shape of a letter tells you how to say it.

How a letter works

Sounds that are related get shapes that are related. Voicing is the clearest case. To turn p into b you do not learn a new letter, you add a bar underneath.

pa
the p sound
no bar, so unvoiced
ba
the b sound
bar underneath means voiced
ta
the t sound
no bar, so unvoiced
da
the d sound
bar underneath means voiced

How a syllable ends

A syllable can end in one of four consonants, and each is a single mark after the vowel. Four is a small number, which is part of why a chorded keyboard for RZ is easy to build.

pan
ending in n
one bar
pas
ending in s
two bars
pal
ending in l
a hook downward
par
ending in r
a tick upward

Grammar suffixes

Endings that carry grammar get their own mark instead of being spelled out. The useful part is that you can see what a verb is doing without reading the whole word.

parla
speaks
plain verb, no suffix mark
parlava
was speaking
past. The -va suffix is the arrow at the end.
parlaria
would speak
conditional. Same verb, fork instead of arrow.
rapidemente
quickly
-mente makes an adverb. It gets its own block.
construccion
construction
-cion makes a noun.
