(the 22-body adopted MIS from lexgen). Soft preferences applied per
beauty-brief §1: (1) mnemonic hooks (Romance/international/Esperanto
evocation within our 10-onset inventory — no f r b d g v z to hook
with), (2) euphony (sonorants to high-frequency), euphony breaking
ties among adequate hooks. Class = POS-alternation class
(gf-grammar.md §2).

Method notes: hooks must survive the 10-onset funnel (no f r b d g
v z) — Romance hooks with those consonants either remap (v→w:
vento→weto, vite→wite; qu→k: quitar→kita) or die (forte, calor→kalo
keeps only the k-l skeleton). The funnel is lossy but the survivors
are real. Phonotactic legality (echo-vowel, tosmabru, fake-geminate)
is asserted at assignment time in the NEXT pass — this seed is
pre-validation and marked as such.
