Method notes from this pass:

- The false-friend screen fired twice more (`vider`→`veder` vs FR
  *vider* "empty"; `cercar` excluded near IT *circa*). Screening is
  earning its mandatory status.
- The EN-Latinate tie-break resolved ~8 entries (bever, encontrar,
  moneta, via, citate, audir, lacte, anno) — it is doing real work,
  not decoration.
- The FLAGged rows (~12) are precisely the cloze-test items to
  oversample: they are the recipe's genuine uncertainty, where data
  must beat taste.
- Spelling correction adopted: `anno` (year), so the news sample's
  `anios` → `annos`.
