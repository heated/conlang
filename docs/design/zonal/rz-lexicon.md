## Method observations from this pass

1. The **screen keeps firing**: corte(court), rose(pink), la(article),
   oficina(IT workshop), riso(IT laugh), large(EN big) — six more
   catches. Split-heavy fields (colors, everyday household) are where
   the recipe strains hardest; abstract/Latinate fields are nearly
   free (biblioteca, importante, memoria — zero-cost rows).
2. **The Ibero-weight bites in body/kinship/household** (hermano,
   cabeza, silla, tienda…) where Ibero forms are NOT pan-Romance —
   these fields have the most FLAG density and will dominate the
   cloze failure analysis.
3. `veritate` correction adopted (derivation-family tie-break beats
   the shorter modern forms).
