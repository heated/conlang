A constructed language you have not studied

Le vento del norte e le sol disputava sobre qui era le plus forte, quando un viajator passava, coprite de un manto calde. Les dos accordava que le prime a facer le viajator remover su manto seria considerate le plus forte. Le vento comenzava a soplar con tote su forza, ma quanto plus el soplava, tanto plus le viajator se copriva. Alora le sol comenzava a brilar caldemente, e immediatamente le viajator removeva su manto.

If your reading languages include Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, French, Catalan or Romanian — or if English's Latinate stratum carried you — you just read a language designed so that you would not have to learn it first. This page asks you to measure how much of it you actually got, and then tells you honestly what that number is and is not worth.

What you just read

RZ, a profile of Interlingua

This is RZ, a Romance zonal auxiliary language. It is not the first language built on this idea and does not pretend to be: RZ is a declared profile of Interlingua (IALA, 1951) — Interlingua's grammar and word stock, with a shallower spelling, an Ibero-leaning choice of words, a few grammatical changes, and a set of engineered add-ons. If you already read Interlingua, you already read most of this.

What is new is not the language. It is the method: every design difference is a bet to be tested against Interlingua on the same texts, priced in learner-hours, and reverted if it does not pay. Interlingua has claimed at-sight readability for seventy years without, as far as this project could find, a published number; no Romance-zone auxiliary language has one. Only Interslavic, in the Slavic zone, does. The point of this page is to start producing that number.

What this number is not. It is one reader, self-administered, auto-scored by string matching against a list of accepted answers — the real instrument uses human semantic scoring, blind subjects with no prior exposure, and n ≥ 30 per source language. Your score is a demonstration of the mechanism, not evidence about it. RZ has no measured comprehension number, and neither, as far as this project's survey found, does Interlingua.

Two published numbers, always

Why specifics and gist are scored separately

Readers routinely report that they "kind of knew what was going on" while failing most of the blanks — a distinct state from reading, and one a single blended percentage hides. A reader inside the zone should score high on both. A reader reached only through English's Latinate vocabulary should show high gist, low specifics. If in-zone readers ever pattern gist-only, the lexicon is failing the zone it was built for, and that is a design bug the instrument is there to catch.

The benchmark is Interslavic, the one zonal language in any family with a published figure: 84% mean cloze comprehension from readers with zero study. That is the precedent for the mechanism. It is not RZ's number, and quoting it as though it were would be exactly the move this project exists to stop making.

Three registers

NarrativeLe tren de les sete
Cata matina, Maria prendeva le tren de les sete a le citate. Ma hodie, quando ela arivava al stacion, le tren no stava ala. «Por que le tren no veniva?» ela demandava al empleate. «Un problema tecnico», el respondeva sin la mirar. «Le proxime va partir a les octo e quinze.» Maria no voleva atender un hora. Ela telefonava a su oficina: «Io va arivar tarde. No, no es grave — io pote laborar durante le viaje.»
InstructionalSopa de tomate
Corta dos cebolas e tres dentes de alio. Calenta un poc de oleo en un pote grande. Ajunta les cebolas e coce cinco minutos. Ajunta le alio, ses tomates cortates, sal e pepe. Coce vinte minutos a foco basse. Al fin, mescla tote ben e serve calde, con pan.
FableLe pastor e le lupo
Un jove pastor guardava su oves proxime del vilage. Por se divertir, el critava: «Lupo! Lupo!» Les vilageses veniva a le ajutar, ma no encontrava necun lupo. Le pastor rideva. El repeteva le joco tres veses. Alora un lupo veniva realmente. Le pastor critava con tote su forza, ma nadie veniva plus. Le lupo comeva multe oves.
Qui menti sempre, no es credite quando dice le veritate.

Profile, not fork

What RZ changes about Interlingua

Roughly 80–85% of RZ's grammar is Interlingua's, form for form: the articles, the -e adjectives, the -te participles, person-invariant verbs, the -va past, the whole derivational suffix family, the pronouns, the calendar. The differences are few, deliberate, and each one is a claim that can lose.

Interlingua (baseline)

RZ (profile)
Le vento del norte e le sol disputava sobre qui era le plus forte, quando un viajator passava, coprite de un manto calde.

Illustrative, not authoritative. The Interlingua column is this project's own rendering, written to show where the deltas fall, and it has not yet been checked against the Interlingua dictionary or by an Interlingua reader. If you read Interlingua and it is wrong, that is worth telling us. A deterministic converter is planned precisely so this comparison stops depending on anyone's hand.

Surface deltas — shallower spelling (-cion for -tion, no Greek digraphs, no doubled consonants), Ibero-leaning word choices, adjective plural agreement, an analytic future, negative concord. Each is a bet that it improves comprehension for the zone; each reverts to the Interlingua form if a measurement says otherwise — and none has been measured yet.

Add-ons — a dense number-and-date mode with a checksum, an aviation-style repair register for noisy conversation, a grammar that declares a learner's most likely mistakes correct rather than wrong, a closed inventory of under a hundred function words, chorded input, an optional display script. These leave ordinary text untouched. They are patterns, not drop-in parts: running them on Interlingua itself would take a fresh implementation and a fresh price.
